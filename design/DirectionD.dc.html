Why
Because the ticket asks whether these are two things and the code has already answered no — one component, one draft, one set of save adapters, split only at the wrapper. This finishes the sentence instead of leaving it half-said, and it deletes the flag that exists purely to stop the page double-rendering. It also gives the grid the thing it does not have: a way to see an item's state without committing to a surface at all.
Tradeoff
A single component that must be correct at 280px, 420px and 1100px is how a file reaches 2,635 lines, not how it leaves. A density prop is the classic next step on that road: every section gains three variants, every test gains three cases, and the honest work of deciding what to cut is postponed by giving it somewhere to hide. It is also the only direction that makes the surface a navigation problem — three states with two transitions the user has to learn.
Sidebar and full page: one thing, three sizes
