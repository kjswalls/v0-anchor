What speaks today
Two systems, both anchored to the bottom of the sidebar, 8px apart — a persistent stack inside the dock capsule and a transient toast floating above it.
The dock stack — persists until answered
Couldn't reach your items BLOCKED 90 until retried
4 items waiting from yesterday DECISION 50 until triaged
Close out Tuesday DECISION 50 until closed
Moved 3 overdue items to today RECEIPT 30 until dismissed
Capped at 2 rows, then folds into “+N more”. Every row that arrives or leaves moves the omnibar underneath it.
The toast — five seconds, then gone
Complete Uncomplete Delete task Delete habit Delete project Skip habit Reset habit Reset streak Schedule Unschedule Move to… Move all tasks Add to… Remove from… Role changed Bulk paste Bulk complete Bulk delete
Eighteen action families, all carrying the same one-press Undo — the app’s single largest source of speech, and the one place it says nothing twice.
The overlap. A receipt notice (“Moved 3 overdue items · Undo”) and an undo toast (“Completed: Morning pages · Undo”) are the same sentence in two different chrome treatments, rendered 8px apart, with different lifetimes and different dismiss gestures.
