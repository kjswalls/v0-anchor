Free, not built

Cross-pane search, ?focus=keys.undo deep links, per-row reset, the modified bar, and unavailable() — all already in SettingRecord.

Why

The settings surface was built so a setting is a record, not markup — that is the whole reason it can search, count, deep-link and reset. Nineteen bindings are nineteen records, generated by a shortcutRecords() spread exactly the way channelRecords() already generates every extension's fields. It also fixes a lie: the rail advertises a Keyboard pane whose one record is a button.

Tradeoff

⌘/ becomes a navigation. You leave the planner to rebind a key about the planner, and you cannot rehearse: AppShell is not mounted on /settings, so most of what you just bound does nothing on the page you bound it from. Two of the three "why" gains are also gains for a surface nobody visits — someone hunting for a key hits ⌘/ before they think of Settings.

The dead key

Solved by a field that already exists. unavailable(ctx) is documented as "present but not actionable, with the reason shown inline" — exactly the posture the extension work asked for. Nothing new is invented.

The one real fit problem

ControlKind is a closed union and read/write are string | boolean. A binding is string[]. Either add a 7th control kind, or store it as "mod+shift+." — in which case isModified, the reset button and the search index all work untouched.

Buildable. Record ids (keys.undo) are new and independent of the frozen shortcut ids; the override store and its migrate never move.
