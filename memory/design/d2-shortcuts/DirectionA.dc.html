Why

⌘/ is a shortcut about shortcuts. The person opening it is mid-flow on the canvas, and a route navigation is a mode change: it throws away the view whose bindings they are reasoning about. A dialog keeps the app underneath, which is also the only way to rehearse — press the key you just recorded and watch the thing behind the scrim move.

Tradeoff

It builds a second search, a second reset affordance and a second "this row is modified" marker next to the ones lib/settings/search.ts and SettingRow already implement, scored and tested. Two implementations of the same three ideas, drifting from here on. And it leaves Settings › Keyboard as a room with one button in it — the dead room stays dead.

The dead key

Rows read isAvailable(ctx) — the same call the dispatcher makes — and say why in the row's own voice: a state reason (honey) reads as "not now", an extension reason (coral) reads as "not switched on" and links to its pane. The key is never taken away; off is inert, not erased.

Watch out

A filter field is a text input, and isFocusedOnInput() withholds every bare binding while one has focus. Rehearsing N or V means clicking out of the filter first. Every direction with a search box inherits this.

Buildable as-is. No registry change, no store change. One component, one selector for availability. The cheapest direction on this canvas by a wide margin.
